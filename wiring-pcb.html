Notes

  • Why 3.3V-only matters here: the planned battery supply is a TP4056 charger feeding a 3.3V buck-boost with an EN-pin switch — there's no 5V rail in that chain at all, so anything still wired to VIN would simply stop working once off USB power.
  • Current trade-off: at 3.3V the single 27Ω resistor gives roughly 55–65mA through the IR LED, in the same range as the breadboard's measured ≈60mA — the rewiring doesn't cost range, it just removes the 5V dependency.
  • Same transistor, same ceiling: BC548B still applies, ~100mA absolute max collector current.
  • Verify against your exact board: pin groupings here follow GPIO number, not a confirmed physical silkscreen order — cross-check before finalizing copper traces.
