⚠ Bar array: all 7 wires land on new pins, in order. a→GPIO22, b→GPIO21, c→GPIO19, d→GPIO18, e→GPIO5, f→GPIO17, g→GPIO16 — reading straight down the 3.3V column. Only b and c are actually crossing sides (from GPIO13/14); a, d, e, f, g were already on the right column, just not in this tidy order, so re-terminating them costs nothing extra once you're laying PCB traces instead of moving jumper wires. Switch, buzzer, RGB, and IR stay exactly where they are.

About GPIO1/3/2/15

Shown as free pins here, not flagged off. Worth knowing what they normally do if you ever reassign them: GPIO1/GPIO3 are the USB serial TX/RX lines — mpremote and the REPL use them, so claiming them for something else means losing your serial console. GPIO2 and GPIO15 are read once at boot to select strapping/boot modes; usually fine to reuse afterward, just double-check your specific board's boot behavior if you do. Nothing stops you from using any of the four — they're just the ones worth a second look before you commit a wire to them.
