Breadboard Wiring Map

The wiring as currently built and reflected in hardware.py — 13 GPIOs across switch, buzzer, RGB status LED, 7‑segment bar, and the IR bulb driver. VIN (5V, from USB) is used once, to give the IR LED more current than the 3.3V rail alone could supply through a bare GPIO.

Notes

  • Measured, not just calculated: the IR resistor pack (5×220Ω ≈ 44Ω) reads ≈60mA in practice at VIN’s 5V, below the ~80mA the plain Ohm’s-law figure suggests — likely some combination of USB cable/connector drop and the BC548B’s VCE(sat). Worth measuring VIN under load if chasing that gap.
  • BC548B ceiling: ~100mA absolute max collector current. The IR branch sits comfortably under that; don’t add a second LED to this same transistor without upgrading it.
  • Physical pin order on the left/right of the diagram is grouped by function, not necessarily left-to-right on your exact board’s silkscreen — check your specific DevKit variant before routing a PCB from this.
  • Companion page: PCB Wiring Map — the same circuit re-pinned for easier PCB layout, with the IR driver moved off VIN entirely so the board runs on 3.3V only (matches the TP4056 + buck-boost battery plan, which never provides a 5V rail).
